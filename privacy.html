Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

FreeMemeAI ("FreeMemeAI", "we", "us", or "our") provides online tools for creating memes and GIF memes. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website and how we use and protect that information.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is necessary to operate and improve the website.

  • Contact information: If you contact us through our contact form, we may receive information such as your name, email address, and the message you submit.
  • Uploaded content: When you upload an image or GIF to use with an editor, the file may be processed by your browser and/or the services required to provide the requested feature. Do not upload confidential, private, or sensitive information.
  • Usage and technical information: Our hosting, security, analytics, advertising, and third-party services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, approximate location, referring page, and requests made to the service.
  • Cookies and local storage: The website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences, provide functionality, measure usage, and serve or measure advertising where applicable.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve FreeMemeAI and its editing tools.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Protect the website against abuse, fraud, spam, and security threats.
  • Understand website usage and improve performance and user experience.
  • Display, personalize, measure, and limit advertising where advertising is enabled and permitted.

3. Uploaded Images and GIFs

FreeMemeAI is designed to let users create memes and GIF memes from content they choose. Uploaded content should not contain passwords, financial information, government identification, private communications, or other sensitive personal information.

Where an editor can process a file directly in your browser, the file may remain in the browser session and may not be uploaded to our servers. Some features may require a third-party service or server-side processing. Because the exact processing path can depend on the feature and browser, users should treat uploads as potentially accessible to the services necessary to provide that feature.

Generated files may be downloaded to your device. We do not claim ownership of user-created content.
