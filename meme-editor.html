Free Meme Generator for Image Memes

Meme Studio is a browser-based meme generator that makes it simple to create image memes from templates or your own pictures. Use the free meme generator workflow to add multiple captions, resize and position text, apply styles, add stickers, draw on the canvas, and customize backgrounds before exporting your meme.

Browse popular, classic, and reaction formats when you want a quick starting point. If you are looking for a Drake meme generator, search the available meme templates for a Drake format and open it in the editor. You can also pick a template at random when you want a random meme generator style of experience.

Some people search for an AI meme generator, but Meme Studio keeps the editing process in your hands. You choose the image, write the joke, control the layout, and decide exactly how the final meme looks. It works on desktop and mobile and supports dark mode for a comfortable editing experience.
